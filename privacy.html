Privacy

What this site knows about you

Very little, by design.

Nothing you enter is kept

The diagnostic scores your answers inside your own browser. They are never sent anywhere, never stored, and never included in any measurement. If you share a result, the answers travel only inside the link you choose to send.

No cookies

This site sets no cookies and writes nothing to your device, with one exception you control: if you opt out of measurement below, your browser remembers that choice.

What is measured

The site may use Google Analytics 4 in a cookieless mode to count page views and a few actions: starting and finishing the diagnostic, the level reached, downloading a sample canvas, and following a link to a store. Advertising features and Google signals are switched off. IP addresses are anonymised. Because no identifier is stored, repeat visits cannot be linked together.

Local previews of the site are never measured.

Opting out

You can stop this browser from being measured on this site, permanently.

Opt out of measurement Opt back in

Third parties

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on every page.
  • Google Analytics receives the measurements described above, if enabled.
  • Notion Press and Amazon handle any purchase. Their own privacy terms apply once you leave this site.
  • The author's contact form on sanjeev-azad.pages.dev receives any note you choose to send, including requests for the canvas pack.

No mailing list

There is no newsletter and no list to join. A request for the canvas pack is answered personally and your address is not added to anything.

Illustrations

The illustrations on this site and in the book were created by the author using AI image tools, in the style of an aged engraving. They depict archetypes, not real people, and no illustration portrays any deity.
