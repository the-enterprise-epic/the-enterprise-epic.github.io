Eighteen canvases, one for every Adhyaya

Every chapter of the book ends in a workshop. Its canvas turns the chapter's idea into something a team completes together, around one real decision, in a single sitting.

One decision

Each canvas starts from a decision your organisation actually faces, never from a dataset or a dashboard.

One sitting

The steps are short prompts. A team works through them together and writes its answers on the page.

One Blueprint

Each canvas adds a layer to the same Enterprise Sensemaking Blueprint, so the work accumulates across the book.
