Enter the EPIC Sabha

Eighteen canvases, one for every chapter

In the book, every chapter closes in a workshop called the EPIC Sabha. Its canvas is the worksheet: it turns the chapter's idea into something a team completes together, around one real decision.

One decision

Each canvas starts from a decision your organisation actually faces, never from a dataset or a dashboard.

One working session

The steps are short prompts. A team works through them together and writes its answers on the page.

One Blueprint

Each canvas adds a layer to the same Enterprise Sensemaking Blueprint, so the work builds across the book.
